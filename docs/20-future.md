# 20 - Future

Everything in this document is speculative and explicitly not committed. It exists to show where the long-term vision ([01-vision.md](01-vision.md)) could lead if the core product proves itself first - not as a backlog. Nothing here should be built before Phases 0–4 of [16-roadmap.md](16-roadmap.md) are done and have actually validated the assumptions in [07-user-research.md](07-user-research.md).

## Directions worth exploring later

**Multi-modal memory.** Beyond text and voice, a future version could let someone add a photo, a voice memo, or a specific date to what Haven remembers - not as a media-sharing feature, but as richer input to the same emotional memory graph. This only makes sense once the text-based memory system has years of proven value; adding modalities to an unproven core would be building depth on an unvalidated foundation.

**Family legacy mode.** An opt-in, carefully bounded feature where someone could choose to leave a curated portion of their remembered story to someone else - a form of the long-term vision in [01-vision.md](01-vision.md) extended beyond one person's lifetime. This raises real consent, ownership, and grief-related design questions (what happens to someone's memory record after they've died, who has any right to access it, how to prevent this from becoming morbid or exploitative) that would need their own dedicated research phase, not a feature sprint.

**Couples' or family shared memory.** A space where two people who both use Haven individually could, with mutual and explicit consent, share a *curated* subset of their own reflections with each other - not a joint account, not visibility into each other's private conversations. This is meaningfully different from and much riskier than the social features we've permanently excluded ([10-feature-list.md](10-feature-list.md)); it would need its own consent architecture and is not a variant of the excluded social/feed features, but a distinct proposal requiring its own scrutiny.

**Clinical-adjacent partnerships.** Beyond the individual therapist bridge in [11-product-prd.md](11-product-prd.md) §4, a longer-term possibility is structured (opt-in, person-initiated) integration with mental health providers or platforms - always as a bridge the person controls, never as a B2B distribution channel, per the reasoning in [17-business-model.md](17-business-model.md) against employer-mediated access and referral-fee conflicts of interest.

**Life-stage-specific companions.** Distinct pacing and question styles for major transitions - grief, becoming a parent, retirement, immigration - building on the life-transition modes already planned for Phase 4. Further out, this could mean deeper specialization, but only ever as modes within one continuous memory, never as separate products that would fragment someone's long-term record.

## What stays fixed regardless of how far this goes

However far Haven's feature set expands, three things from the mission ([02-mission.md](02-mission.md)) do not get revisited as the company grows, and this document is not license to reopen them: no advertising or sale of conversation data, no design for parasocial or romantic dependency, and no feature that makes Haven harder to leave than it is to stay. If a future opportunity would require bending one of these to pursue, the opportunity gets declined, not the principle.
