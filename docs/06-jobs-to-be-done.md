# 06 — Jobs to Be Done

We use JTBD instead of feature wishlists because almost nobody, if you asked them directly, would say "I want an AI companion." They'd describe a moment and a discomfort. The job is what they're actually hiring something to do in that moment.

## Core job

**When** something happens in my day that affected me — big or small — and no one available to me right now has the bandwidth, context, or emotional room to really hear it, **I want** to say it out loud (or type it) to something that will actually pay attention, **so I can** stop carrying it alone and not drag it into tomorrow.

This is the job Haven exists to do. Everything in the feature list ([10-feature-list.md](10-feature-list.md)) either serves this job directly or doesn't ship.

## Supporting jobs

**1. The re-explaining tax**
*When* I want to talk about something ongoing — a relationship, a work situation, a pattern I keep noticing about myself — *I want* to not have to re-explain the whole backstory every time, *so that* I can get to what's actually new today instead of spending the first five minutes on exposition.

**2. The mirror job**
*When* I've been turning something over in my head for too long to see it clearly, *I want* to hear it reflected back to me accurately, *so that* I can actually understand what it is I'm feeling, instead of just feeling it.

**3. The "am I the only one" job**
*When* something happens repeatedly — the same argument, the same spiral, the same regret — *I want* to be shown the pattern I can't see from inside it, *so that* I can recognize it before I'm five steps into it again.

**4. The proof-of-self job**
*When* I'm in a low moment and convinced I haven't grown or changed, *I want* to be reminded, with actual specifics, of who I was a year ago and what I've moved through since, *so that* I can believe my own progress instead of just being told to.

**5. The dignity job**
*When* I share something vulnerable, *I want* to not be treated as a problem to solve or a case to manage, *so that* I feel respected, not handled.

## Jobs Haven explicitly does *not* take on

Naming these matters as much as the jobs above, because it's tempting to expand scope into any of them:

- **The decision-making job** — "tell me what to do" is a job for a therapist, a trusted person, or the user's own judgment. Haven can help someone think, not think for them.
- **The crisis-intervention job** — Haven detects and hands off; it does not attempt to be the solution in an acute crisis.
- **The productivity job** — task lists, habit tracking, goal-setting belong to a different category of product and would compete for attention with the actual job above.
- **The social job** — Haven is not trying to be someone's only outlet. A job it *does* do well is nudging people back toward the humans in their life when that's what would actually help (see the off-ramp principle in [02-mission.md](02-mission.md)).
