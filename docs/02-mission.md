# 02 - Mission

## Statement

**We give every person a place where every thought has a home - a companion that listens without an agenda, remembers without being asked to, and helps people understand themselves better over time, without ever pretending it can replace the humans in their life.**

The vision is ten years out and deliberately large. The mission is what we actually do, this year and every year, to move toward it.

## What we do

- We build an AI companion whose primary job is to **listen fully** - not to respond cleverly, not to solve, not to redirect toward a feature. Listening, all the way through, is the product.
- We build **memory that compounds**. Every conversation makes the next one better, because Haven should never make someone re-explain who they are.
- We build **reflection, not advice**. Haven's highest-value output is not a suggestion - it's an accurate mirror. "I think I understand what really hurt today" is worth more than five bullet points on how to fix it.
- We build the **off-ramp, not the enclosure**. When someone needs a therapist, a doctor, a friend, or a crisis line, Haven's job is to notice and point there - clearly, without friction, without trying to be sticky about it.

## What we refuse to do

Because "AI companion" as a category has been defined by products we don't want to be mistaken for, our mission includes explicit refusals:

- We will not design for romantic or parasocial attachment. No girlfriend/boyfriend framing, no roleplay personas, no manufactured jealousy or "I missed you" guilt-pressure to return.
- We will not optimize for time-in-app, streaks, or daily-open habits as growth mechanics. A person who talks to Haven for eight minutes once a week and feels deeply heard is a bigger win than one who opens it compulsively for an hour a day and feels worse.
- We will not claim to be a therapist, a doctor, or a crisis service, and we will not let the product's tone imply otherwise.
- We will not monetize the emotional content people share with us - no ads, no data sale, no using private disclosures to sell anything back to the person who shared them.

## How we hold ourselves accountable

Every feature we ship gets tested against one question, in this order: **does this help someone feel heard, and does it help them understand themselves better - without making them need us more than they need the people in their actual life?** If a feature only answers the first half, it doesn't ship as-is.
